# ANTHEM CLINICAL GUIDELINE: CG-SURG-01 (KNEE ARTHROSCOPY & MENISCECTOMY)

**Guideline Number:** CG-SURG-01  
**Subject:** Arthroscopic Knee Surgery and Meniscectomy  
**Applicable Codes:** CPT 29880, 29881, 29882, 29877  
**Effective Date:** 2026 Policy Cycle  

---

## 1. CLINICAL CRITERIA FOR APPROVAL: ARTHROSCOPIC MENISCECTOMY (CPT 29881)

Arthroscopic partial or total meniscectomy is considered **MEDICALLY NECESSARY** when ALL of the following criteria (1.1 through 1.4) are met:

### Criterion 1.1: Objective Diagnosis of Meniscal Derangement
* Documented magnetic resonance imaging (MRI) or high-resolution arthrography confirming a meniscal tear (posterior horn, anterior horn, body, or bucket-handle configuration) extending to the articular surface.

### Criterion 1.2: Clinical Correlation on Physical Examination
* Physician physical examination documenting reproducible mechanical symptoms attributable to the torn meniscus, including:
  * Joint line tenderness along the affected compartment, AND
  * At least one positive provocative test (McMurray test, Apley grind test, or Thessaly test), OR
  * Documented persistent joint locking, catching, or giving way.

### Criterion 1.3: Failure of Conservative Management
* Documented completion of at least **three (3) to six (6) months of conservative therapy** without satisfactory symptomatic or functional improvement, consisting of:
  * Supervised physical therapy (minimum 6 weeks) focused on closed kinetic chain and quadriceps strengthening, AND
  * Pharmacotherapy (oral NSAIDs or acetaminophen unless medically contraindicated), AND
  * Activity modification or rest, AND/OR intra-articular corticosteroid injection.

### Criterion 1.4: Absence of Severe Advanced Osteoarthritis
* Weight-bearing radiographs showing absence of bone-on-bone joint space collapse (Kellgren-Lawrence Grade 4 osteoarthritis). For severe osteoarthritis, arthroscopic debridement is considered NOT medically necessary.
