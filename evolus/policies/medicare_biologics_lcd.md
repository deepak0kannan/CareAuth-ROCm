# MEDICARE LOCAL COVERAGE DETERMINATION (LCD) L33777: TARGETED BIOLOGICS & IMMUNOMODULATORS

**LCD Identifier:** LCD L33777  
**Contractor:** Centers for Medicare & Medicaid Services (CMS) / Jurisdiction J-M  
**Target Therapies:** Adalimumab (J0135), Infliximab (J1745), Mepolizumab (J2185), Rituximab (J9312)  
**Indications:** Rheumatoid Arthritis, Eosinophilic Granulomatosis with Polyangiitis, Psoriatic Arthritis  

---

## SECTION 1: COVERAGE INDICATIONS FOR RHEUMATOID ARTHRITIS (ADALIMUMAB / HUMIRA)

Adalimumab is covered for moderate-to-severe Rheumatoid Arthritis (ICD-10 M05.xxx / M06.xxx) when criteria 1.1 through 1.3 are verified:

### Criterion 1.1: Objective Disease Activity
* Positive serologies (Rheumatoid Factor $>14$ IU/mL or Anti-CCP $>20$ U/mL) AND elevated inflammatory markers (ESR $>30$ mm/hr or CRP $>10$ mg/L) with documented tender/swollen joint count $\ge 6$.

### Criterion 1.2: Mandatory Step-Therapy Protocol (The csDMARD Rule)
* Documented prior trial of at least **three (3) consecutive months** of at least one conventional synthetic disease-modifying anti-rheumatic drug (csDMARD) at maximally tolerated standard doses:
  * **First-line requirement:** Oral or subcutaneous Methotrexate (15–25 mg weekly), OR
  * Leflunomide (20 mg daily) or Sulfasalazine (2–3 g daily).
* **Exception:** If patient has a documented severe medical contraindication to Methotrexate (e.g., active hepatic cirrhosis, eGFR $<30$, bone marrow suppression, pregnancy), this criterion may be waived upon explicit diagnostic verification.

### Criterion 1.3: Pre-Treatment Infectious Screening
* Documented negative tuberculosis screening (Quantiferon-TB Gold or PPD) and negative Hepatitis B surface antigen within the preceding 12 months.

---

## SECTION 4: EXEMPTIONS & EMERGENCY CARVE-OUTS

### Section 4.2.1: Emergency Step-Therapy Exemption for Organ- or Life-Threatening Manifestations
* Mandatory step-therapy trials, multi-week trial waiting periods, and prior-authorization delays are strictly **WAIVED** when the treating specialist certifies that immediate biologic intervention is required to arrest acute, irreversible organ deterioration or imminent mortality.
* **Eligible Conditions:**
  * Documented systemic vasculitic crises (e.g., EGPA / Churg-Strauss with cardiac or acute renal failure).
  * Acute eosinophilic myocarditis or acute pulmonary hemorrhage.
  * Rapid progressive necrotizing glomerulonephritis.
* In such events, retrospective approval and coverage for emergency transport and specialty biologic infusion shall be granted upon receipt of concurrent clinical telemetry, cardiac biomarkers, and ICU transfer documentation.
